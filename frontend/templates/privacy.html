Privacy

Your palm, handled with care

Palm images can be sensitive, biometric-like information. PalmStory AI is built privacy-first.

What happens to your palm image

By default your palm photo is used only to create your reading, then deleted. We store the reading and comic — not the raw image — unless you explicitly turn on retention in Settings.

What we never do

We never train models on your images, never sell or share them, and never put images in logs or error reports.

Your controls

You can view your history, delete a single reading, delete everything, export your data, and delete your account — which also removes any stored images.

Entertainment, not prediction

Readings are AI-generated, fictionalized storytelling based on visible features. Palmistry is a cultural tradition, not a scientifically validated method of prediction, and nothing here is medical, financial, legal, or psychological advice.

This page states the intended model; the enforcing code lands in Phase 13. A full policy will accompany launch.
